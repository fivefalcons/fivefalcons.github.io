01 / Your opportunity

Indicate your preferences. This qualification application does not establish contractor status or replace an operating agreement.

02 / Applicant information

Carrier: Five Falcons Express Inc, 501 Fox Hill Circle Apt G, Bloomington, IL 61701. Sensitive details will only be collected after secure submission is configured.

03 / Licenses and experience

Include every unexpired commercial motor vehicle license or permit. Also identify licensing authorities held during the past three years for the motor vehicle record inquiry.

04 / Safety history

Report the preceding three years, including personal and commercial vehicles. Enter None when applicable.

05 / Work history

List all employers from the past three years, including non-driving work, and commercial motor vehicle employers for the additional seven years. Include self-employment/contract assignments. Use as many entries as needed; do not include prior compensation.

06 / DOT testing history

Under 49 CFR 40.25(j), answer for DOT-covered safety-sensitive transportation positions you applied for but did not obtain during the past two years.

07 / Safety-history notice and your rights

Information supplied about previous employment may be used to investigate your safety performance history with DOT-regulated employers during the preceding three years under 49 CFR 391.23.

You may review information provided by previous employers, ask a previous employer to correct errors and send corrected information to Five Falcons, and submit a rebuttal to that employer if you cannot agree on accuracy.

To review these records, send a written request to Five Falcons Express Inc at the carrier address above or info@fivefalconsexpress.com. Requests may be made when applying or within 30 days after being hired or notified of a denial. Records will be provided within five business days after the request or after we receive them, whichever is later. If you do not arrange to pick up or receive them within 30 days after we make them available, your right to review them is waived. Previous employers have 15 days to correct and forward information or notify you that they decline; rebuttals must be forwarded as required by 49 CFR 391.23.

Separate authorization will be provided for consumer reports, where applicable. A pre-employment full Clearinghouse query requires your electronic consent inside the FMCSA Clearinghouse. Signing this application does not supply that consent.

08 / Review and electronic signature

This certifies that this application was completed by me, and that all entries on it and information in it are true and complete to the best of my knowledge.

Electronic-record consent, a downloadable signed copy, verification and the signature audit record must be supplied by the secure signing service before this application can be accepted. A paper alternative will be available through recruiting.
